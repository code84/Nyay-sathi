## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.13(3): "The notice referred to in sub-section (2) shall give details of the amount payable by the borrower and the secured assets intended to be enforced by the secured creditor in the event of non-payment of secured debts by the borrower"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
A proper SARFAESI notice must state how much is payable and which secured assets the lender intends to enforce.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
This is only the minimum content named in s.13(3). It does not say what else a valid notice needs. The bot should flag a missing amount or missing asset details as a point to raise with a lawyer, not as proof that the notice is invalid.
