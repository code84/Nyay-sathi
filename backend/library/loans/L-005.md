## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.17(1): "Any person (including borrower), aggrieved by any of the measures referred to in sub-section (4) of section 13 taken by the"
s.17(1): "to the Debts Recovery Tribunal having jurisdiction in the matter within forty-five days from the date on which such measure had been taken"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
A borrower (or anyone else) harmed by a measure taken under s.13(4) can apply to the Debts Recovery Tribunal within 45 days from the date the measure was taken.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
The 45 days run from when the MEASURE was taken, not from the 60-day notice. This is the most time-critical rule here, so the bot must show it with the quoted text, calculate dates in code, and say 'see a lawyer now'. Whether a late application can be heard is not covered by this quote.
