## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.31: "The provisions of this Act shall not apply to"
s.31(j): "any case in which the amount due is less than twenty per cent. of the principal amount and interest thereon"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
The Act does not apply to several kinds of cases, including a case where the amount due is less than 20% of the principal amount and interest.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Other exclusions in s.31 (liens, pledges of movables, certain agricultural-related and other items) are not quoted. Do not tell a user the Act does or does not apply to them; say it depends and a lawyer should check.
