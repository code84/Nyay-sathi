## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.18(1): "an acknowledgment of liability in respect of such property or right has been made in writing signed by the party against whom such property or right is claimed"
s.18(1): "a fresh period of limitation shall be computed from the time when the acknowledgment was so signed"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
If, before the time limit runs out, the person who owes the money signs a written acknowledgment of the liability, a new limitation period starts from the date it was signed.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
The acknowledgment must be made BEFORE the old period expires and must be in writing and signed. Whether a particular message or email counts is a question for a lawyer, so the bot must not decide it.
