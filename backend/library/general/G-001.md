## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.3(1): "every suit instituted, appeal preferred, and application made after the prescribed period shall be dismissed, although limitation has not been set up as a defence"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
A suit filed after the time limit has to be dismissed by the court even if the other side never raises the time limit.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Subject to sections 4 to 24, which can extend or pause the time. This is the general rule; the length of the period depends on the type of claim (see G-005).
