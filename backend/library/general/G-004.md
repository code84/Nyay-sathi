## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.19: "Where payment on account of a debt or of interest on a legacy is made before the expiration of the prescribed period by the person liable to pay the debt or legacy or by his agent duly authorised in this behalf, a fresh period of limitation shall be computed from the time when the payment was made"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
If the person who owes a debt pays part of it before the time limit ends, a fresh limitation period runs from the date of that payment.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
A proviso (not quoted) requires an acknowledgment of the payment in writing signed by, or in the handwriting of, the payer, in most cases. Do not tell a user a payment definitely restarted the clock.
