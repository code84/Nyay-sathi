## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
Art. 19: "For money payable for money lent"
Art. 19: "When the loan is made"
Art. 55: "For compensation for the breach of any contract, express or implied not herein specially provided for"
Art. 113: "Any suit for which no period of limitation is provided elsewhere in this Schedule"
Art. 113: "When the right to sue accrues"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
Money lent: 3 years from when the loan is made. Compensation for breach of contract: 3 years from when the contract is broken. Anything with no specific period: 3 years from when the right to sue accrues.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
The Schedule has many other articles with different periods. The 'Three years' column is not machine-quoted here (table layout); confirm it by reading the Schedule page. Do not apply these figures to cheque bounce (its own deadlines are in N-004), consumer complaints (C-005), or loans enforced under SARFAESI. Time can be extended or restarted (G-002 to G-004).
