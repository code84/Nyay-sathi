## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.5: "may be admitted after the prescribed period if the appellant or the applicant satisfies the court that he had sufficient cause for not preferring the appeal or making the application within such period"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
For an APPEAL or APPLICATION (not an ordinary suit), the court may accept it late if the person shows a good reason for the delay.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Section 5 covers appeals and applications, not suits, and excludes certain execution applications. The court decides; delay is never guaranteed to be excused.
