## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.147: "every offence punishable under this Act shall be compoundable"
s.148(1): "the Appellate Court may order the appellant to deposit such sum which shall be a minimum of twenty per cent. of the fine or compensation awarded by the trial Court"
s.148(2): "The amount referred to in sub-section (1) shall be deposited within sixty days from the date of the order"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
Offences under this Act can be settled between the parties (compounded). If the drawer is convicted and appeals, the appeal court may order a deposit of at least 20% of the fine or compensation awarded.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
'Compoundable' does not say how or when compounding works in practice (procedure is not in this text), and settlement may need court permission or lawyer help. The 20% is the minimum deposit if the court orders it. It is the appeal court's choice.
