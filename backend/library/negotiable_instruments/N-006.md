## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.143(1): "all offences under this Chapter shall be tried by a Judicial Magistrate of the first class or by a Metropolitan Magistrate"
s.143(1) proviso: "it shall be lawful for the Magistrate to pass a sentence of imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and an amount of fine exceeding five thousand rupees"
s.143(3): "an endeavour shall be made to conclude the trial within six months from the date of filing of the complaint"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
These cases are tried quickly (summary trial). In a summary trial the Magistrate can sentence up to one year, and the law says the court should try to finish within six months of the complaint being filed.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
The six months is an 'endeavour', not a guarantee: real cases often take longer, and the bot must not promise a timeline. The Magistrate can switch to a regular trial in some cases (second proviso, not quoted).
