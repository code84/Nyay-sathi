## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.142(2): "The offence under section 138 shall be inquired into and tried only by a court within whose local jurisdiction"
s.142(2)(a): "if the cheque is delivered for collection through an account, the branch of the bank where the payee or holder in due course, as the case may be, maintains the account, is situated"
s.142(2)(b): "if the cheque is presented for payment by the payee or holder in due course, otherwise through an account, the branch of the drawee bank where the drawer maintains the account, is situated"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
The case is filed in the court that covers the payee's bank branch (if the cheque was deposited into their account), or the drawer's bank branch (if it was presented otherwise).

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Section 142(2) has an Explanation (about cheques delivered at any branch of the payee's bank) that is not quoted here. Where to file can be technical; recommend a lawyer for filing.
