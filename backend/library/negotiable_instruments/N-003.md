## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.139: "It shall be presumed, unless the contrary is proved, that the holder of a cheque received the cheque"
s.139: "for the discharge, in whole or in part, of any debt or other liability"
s.140: "that the drawer had no reason to believe when he issued the cheque that the cheque may be dishonoured on presentment for the reasons stated in that section"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
The court starts by assuming the cheque was given to pay a debt or liability, unless the drawer proves otherwise. And 'I didn't think it would bounce' is not a defence.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
This is a presumption, not a conclusion: the drawer can try to prove otherwise. It does not say who will win, and the bot must not say so.
