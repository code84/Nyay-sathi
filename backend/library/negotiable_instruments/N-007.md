## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.143A(1): "the Court trying an offence under section 138 may order the drawer of the cheque to pay interim compensation to the complainant"
s.143A(2): "shall not exceed twenty per cent. of the amount of the cheque"
s.143A(3): "The interim compensation shall be paid within sixty days from the date of the order"
s.143A(4): "If the drawer of the cheque is acquitted, the Court shall direct the complainant to repay to the drawer the amount of interim compensation, with interest at the bank rate"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
While the case is going on, the court MAY order the drawer to pay the complainant up to 20% of the cheque amount, within 60 days of the order. If the drawer is later acquitted, the complainant must repay it with interest.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
The court MAY order this; it is not automatic and the percentage is a maximum. The 60 days can be extended by up to 30 days on sufficient cause (sub-section (3), not fully quoted). Never promise the user this money.
