## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.39(1) intro: "it shall issue an order to the opposite party directing him to do one or more of the following"
s.39(1)(b): "to replace the goods with new goods of similar description which shall be free from any defect"
s.39(1)(c): "to return to the complainant the price, or, as the case may be, the charges paid by the complainant along with such interest on such price or charges as may be decided"
s.39(1)(d): "to pay such amount as may be awarded by it as compensation to the consumer for any loss or injury suffered by the consumer due to the negligence of the opposite party"
s.39(1)(d) proviso: "the District Commission shall have the power to grant punitive damages in such circumstances as it deems fit"
s.39(1)(f): "to remove the defects in goods or deficiencies in the services in question"
s.39(1)(m): "to provide for adequate costs to parties"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
IF the Commission is satisfied your complaint is proved, it can order a replacement, return of the price with interest as it decides, compensation for loss or injury due to the seller's negligence, removal of defects or deficiencies, and costs. It can also award punitive damages in some circumstances.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Orders are the Commission's choice; nothing is automatic and amounts are not fixed. The bot must NEVER promise a refund, compensation, interest rate or amount. Other orders in s.39(1) (clauses a, e, g-l, n) are not quoted.
