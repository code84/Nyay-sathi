## Excerpt (copied from the source; partial where noted)
Amends Rule 7(2) of the Consumer Protection (Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions) Rules, 2020; effective on publication (17 Aug 2023). Fee by "value of goods or services paid as consideration":
Upto Rs 5 lakh: Nil
Above 5 lakh to 10 lakh: Rs 200
Above 10 lakh to 20 lakh: Rs 400
Above 20 lakh to 50 lakh: Rs 1000
Above 50 lakh to 1 crore: Rs 2000
Above 1 crore to 2 crore: Rs 2500
Above 2 crore to 4 crore: Rs 3000
Above 4 crore to 6 crore: Rs 4000
Above 6 crore to 8 crore: Rs 5000
Above 8 crore to 10 crore: Rs 6000
Above 10 crore: Rs 7500

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
For most everyday refund cases (paid up to Rs 5 lakh) there is no filing fee.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
This is the version as of Aug 2023. I did not check for later amendments - do that before relying on it. Does not include lawyer fees, courier, or other costs.
