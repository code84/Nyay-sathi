## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.69(1): "shall not admit a complaint unless it is filed within two years from the date on which the cause of action has arisen"
s.69(2): "if the complainant satisfies the District Commission, the State Commission or the National Commission, as the case may be, that he had sufficient cause for not filing the complaint within such period"
s.69(2) proviso: "records its reasons for condoning such delay"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
Generally you have two years from when the cause of action arose (for example, when the refund was refused). A late complaint can still be heard if you show a good reason, but the Commission decides and must record its reasons.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
What date the 'cause of action' arose can be argued. The bot may show a date but must not present it as a guaranteed deadline; if the user is close to two years, say see a lawyer or consumer helpline promptly.
