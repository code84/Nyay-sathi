## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.2(7)(i): "buys any goods for a consideration which has been paid or promised or partly paid and partly promised, or under any system of deferred payment"
s.2(7)(i) exclusion: "but does not include a person who obtains such goods for resale or for any commercial purpose"
s.2(7)(ii): "hires or avails of any service for a consideration which has been paid or promised or partly paid and partly promised, or under any system of deferred payment"
s.2(7)(ii) exclusion: "but does not include a person who avails of such service for any commercial purpose"
s.2(7) Explanation (b): "includes offline or online transactions through electronic means"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
You are a consumer if you paid, or promised to pay, for goods or a service, including when you bought online. You are NOT a consumer for this purpose if you bought the goods for resale or for a commercial purpose.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
The Explanation also says buying goods only to earn your livelihood by self-employment is not 'commercial purpose' (text not quoted here). The definition also covers users and beneficiaries other than the buyer. Whether a particular buyer qualifies can be argued; the bot should not decide it for them.
