## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.2(11): "any fault, imperfection, shortcoming or inadequacy in the quality, nature and manner of performance which is required to be maintained by or under any law for the time being in force or has been undertaken to be performed by a person in pursuance of a contract or otherwise in relation to any service"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
If a service was not performed to the standard the law requires, or as the provider agreed to perform it, that can count as a 'deficiency', which is a ground for complaint.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
This is about SERVICES only. The definition continues with 'includes' (negligence, deliberate withholding of relevant information, etc.), which is not quoted here. Defects in goods are a different definition, not yet in the library.
