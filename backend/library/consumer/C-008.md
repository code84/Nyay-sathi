## Excerpt (copied from the source; partial where noted)
Rule 4(5): the grievance officer "acknowledges the receipt of any consumer complaint within forty-eight hours," and "redresses the complaint within one month from the date of receipt."
Sellers on a marketplace have similar duties (reported as Rule 6(4)(b); Rule 6(5) covers displaying the grievance officer details).

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
Before going to the commission, a buyer on an online platform can complain to the platform's grievance officer and expect an acknowledgement within 48 hours and a resolution within a month.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Rule numbers come from a secondary source; two sources I checked gave inconsistent rule numbers, so confirm the exact numbering in the Gazette. Applies to e-commerce entities and marketplace sellers, not an ordinary shop.
