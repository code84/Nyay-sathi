## Excerpt (verbatim quotes from the source PDF; every quoted fragment is machine-checked)
s.34(2)(a): "the opposite party or each of the opposite parties, where there are more than one, at the time of the institution of the complaint, ordinarily resides or carries on business or has a branch office or personally works for gain"
s.34(2)(b) condition: "provided that in such case the permission of the District Commission is given"
s.34(2)(c): "the cause of action, wholly or in part, arises"
s.34(2)(d): "the complainant resides or personally works for gain"
s.35(1): "may be filed with a District Commission by"
s.35(1) proviso: "the complaint under this sub-section may be filed electronically in such manner as may be prescribed"

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
A complaint can be filed in the District Commission where the seller is based, where the problem arose, or where YOU live or work, so you don't have to go to the seller's city. The Act allows online filing.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Option (b), filing where one of several opposite parties is, needs the Commission's permission. The Act does not name the online portal; that comes from the Rules, not yet verified. The consumer, a recognised consumer association, groups of consumers and governments may file (s.35(1)(a)-(d)).
