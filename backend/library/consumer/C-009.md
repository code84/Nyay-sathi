## Excerpt (copied from the source; partial where noted)
Rule 4(10) (as reported): the entity must "effect all payments towards accepted refund requests of the consumers" "within a reasonable period of time, or as prescribed under applicable laws."

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
Once an online platform accepts a refund request it must pay it within a reasonable time (or the period set by banking rules). The rule does not set a fixed number of days.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
Does not say a platform must accept every refund request, and gives no fixed deadline. One summary site cited this as Rule 4(11); the legitquest page says 4(10). Verify in the Gazette.
