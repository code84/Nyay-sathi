## Excerpt (copied from the source; partial where noted)
"launched by the Department of Consumer Affairs to create awareness, advise and redress consumer grievances."
Toll free 1915 (8AM to 8PM); 1800-11-4000; WhatsApp 8800001915; web portal; NCH and UMANG apps. 1930 is for cyber financial fraud only.

## Plain-language meaning (my words)
A free first step: call or message the helpline and it will try to get the company to resolve your complaint before you go to the commission.

## Limits (what this does NOT say)
The helpline does not decide cases or guarantee a refund. Numbers and hours were read from the portal's About page on the verification date and may change - re-check before release.
